Chapter 6 — Report Card Comments

30 prompts

Twenty-eight comments in one sitting is the job nobody plans for and everybody

dreads. These prompts do the drafting so you can do the judging — each comment

tied to something you actually recorded, with one next step the student can act on.

What's in here

Worth knowing before you start

Use initials or "Student A". These comments end up in a permanent record;

the drafting should not put a name into an AI tool.

Give it your real evidence — scores, dates, observations you wrote down. A

comment built on nothing reads like it was built on nothing.

Do not let it invent a strength to balance the structure. A manufactured

compliment is the fastest way for a family to stop trusting the rest.

Vary the openings. Twenty-eight comments that all begin the same way are

immediately recognisable as generated, and parents compare.

Example — an editorial illustration, not a logged AI run
