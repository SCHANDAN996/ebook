How to use this book

The short version

1. Find the prompt for what you are actually doing right now. 2. Copy everything between COPY FROM HERE and TO HERE. 3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. 4. Fill in the parts in [SQUARE BRACKETS] with your real details. 5. Read what comes back before anyone else does.

That is the whole method. Nothing to install, nothing to learn first.

Filling in the brackets

Every prompt has parts like [GRADE_SUBJECT] or [UNIT_TOPIC]. Replace the whole thing, brackets included.

[GRADE_SUBJECT: exact age/grade and subject]

becomes

Year 7 science

Be specific. "Year 7 science, mixed ability, 26 students, 50-minute lesson, no lab access this week" gets you something usable. "Science" gets you something generic.

If a prompt asks for something you genuinely do not have, say so in the chat rather than inventing it. The prompts are written to ask you back.

Getting a better result

Answer its questions. If it comes back asking for the assessment date or the reading level, the prompt is working correctly. Answer, and the next draft will be much closer.

Ask for a change instead of starting again. "Same plan, but guided practice is too long for 15 minutes" is faster than a fresh prompt.

One task at a time. These prompts are deliberately narrow. Asking for a lesson plan, a worksheet and a parent email in one go gets you three mediocre things.

What the sections mean

When you need this — the situation, so you can tell two similar prompts apart.

Fill in — what you need to hand before you start.

COPY FROM HERE / TO HERE — the prompt. Copy all of it.

Before you send it — what usually goes wrong on *this* kind of task. Short, and specific to this prompt.

Example — where included, an illustration of the shape of a good answer. It is an editorial illustration, not a logged AI run.

Three things this book will not do

It will not know your students. Nothing here should be given identifying details. See the safety page.

It will not check your subject facts. Verify content and calculations yourself, especially anything a student will be marked on.

It will not decide anything. Grades, placements, behaviour outcomes and anything touching safeguarding stay with you and your school's process.

If a prompt does not work

Tell us which one — the ID is at the top of every prompt, like LP-002 — and what you got instead. That is the fastest way to get it fixed.
