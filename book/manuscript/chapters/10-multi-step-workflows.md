# Chapter 10: Multi-Step Teacher Workflows

A workflow connects several prompts into one larger teacher task. This chapter contains 12
chains for unit planning, assessment, report comments, family conversations, differentiation,
substitute packs, projects, conferences, interventions, trips, weekly administration and
class-data reflection.

## The pause-and-review rule

Never run every step blindly. Complete one step, review its facts and correct errors, then
pass that approved output to the next step. If a response is empty, refused, truncated or
misaligned, stop the chain. A later polished document cannot repair a false assumption that
entered at the beginning.

## Before using the final pack

Trace each claim to supplied evidence, confirm internal alignment and remove confidential
information. Check policy, safety, accessibility, dates and owners. The fictional examples
show process only; they are not classroom results or proof that a workflow saves a specific
amount of time. Human approval remains the final step in every chain.
