# Chapter 7: Classroom Management & SEL

Classroom management is strongest when expectations are taught, practised and repaired—not
when consequences are generated in response to frustration. The 25 prompts here support
routines, transitions, behaviour resets, restorative conversations, SEL, morning meetings
and seating or grouping decisions.

## How to use this chapter

Describe observable events and the desired routine. Ask for clear teacher language, student
steps, rehearsal, feedback and follow-up. For restorative conversations, first confirm
safety, readiness, voluntariness and local policy. AI should not decide guilt, diagnose a
learner or write a coercive apology.

## Safety and dignity check

Review every suggestion for age appropriateness, accessibility and cultural humility.
Exclude public shaming, collective punishment and removal of required supports. Serious
safeguarding, discrimination, violence or mental-health concerns require qualified people
and established school procedures; a generated script is never a substitute.
