# Chapter 2: Worksheets & Activities

An attractive worksheet can still be educationally empty. The 40 prompts in this chapter
are designed around purposeful practice: worksheets, stations, collaborative tasks, review
games, homework sets and project briefs. Each resource should move learners toward a named
goal and give the teacher usable evidence—not merely keep a class busy.

## How to use this chapter

Specify what learners already know, the exact skill being practised, the desired number of
items, time available and printing or technology limits. For group activities, state the
class size and require both a shared product and an individual check. For games, avoid
systems where the fastest learner does all the thinking.

## Before printing or sharing

Complete every item yourself. Verify the key, numbering, diagrams and directions. Check
that difficulty rises intentionally and that there is enough writing space. Remove questions
that repeat a procedure without adding insight. If the model invents a source, quotation or
real-world fact, replace it with verified material before classroom use.

## Fictional test case

Grade 6 mathematics; ratio tables; 12 items; easy to moderate; black-and-white printing; larger spacing.

## Sample output

**Illustrative excerpt — Grade 7 ecosystem activity**

**Directions:** Arrange Sunlight → Algae → Snails → Perch → Herons. Remove the snail card,
then annotate every downstream effect with ↑, ↓ or ? and one reason.

1. Which organism loses its immediate food source? Explain.
2. Why can herons be affected even though they do not eat snails?
3. Predict one change to algae and state what additional evidence you would need.

**Answer guide:** Perch decrease because their prey is missing; herons may later decrease
because fewer perch are available. Algae may increase as grazing falls, although a firm
claim requires information about other grazers and limiting factors. Award reasoning,
not the arrow alone. A response saying only perch change reveals a broken-chain
misconception and should receive a short food-web tracing task.
