# WF-009 | Evidence to intervention-plan draft

## Outcome

Organize anonymous learning evidence into a time-bound instructional support draft.

## Teacher inputs

Supply the source goal, verified constraints and only non-identifying evidence needed for the next stage. This workflow's specific input requirements are listed in its numbered stages. Do not paste a cumulative student dossier.

## Workflow

1. Define the exact skill, baseline measure, prior instruction and non-identifying evidence; do not diagnose causes.
2. Analyze patterns, identify a narrow instructional target and state uncertainty or missing information.
3. Choose an evidence-aligned teaching strategy, dosage, materials and implementation owner.
4. Define progress checks, graphing or tally method, review date and decision rules for continue/change/escalate.
5. Audit feasibility, access, policy and data privacy; label the result a draft requiring qualified team approval.

Pass only the necessary previous **reviewed** output, not the entire conversation. Keep a local version label for each approved artifact. If an upstream fact changes, recheck every dependent artifact. Reset context between learner records.

## Fictional end-to-end example

Editorial worked illustration, not a recorded AI run or classroom test.

Inputs: Grade 5; synthetic responses show unit-piece confusion when comparing unlike fractions; two-week proposed instructional trial.

1. Target: compare equal-whole fractions with a justified representation.
2. Strategy: contrast equal strips and missing pieces; no diagnosis.
3. Proposed schedule: two brief classroom practices weekly, subject to teacher capacity.
4. Check fresh comparisons and record explanation evidence separately from arithmetic.
5. Review after the proposed period; teacher chooses continue/change from evidence, not an automatic diagnostic label.
