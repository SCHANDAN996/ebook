# WF-003 | Incident to parent conversation

## Outcome

Move from verified classroom facts to a calm, policy-aligned family conversation.

## Teacher inputs

Supply the source goal, verified constraints and only non-identifying evidence needed for the next stage. This workflow's specific input requirements are listed in its numbered stages. Do not paste a cumulative student dossier.

## Workflow

1. Record observable facts, time, place, roles, immediate safety actions and applicable school procedure.
2. Separate confirmed facts, reported information, interpretation and unknowns; teacher verifies the record.
3. Draft a neutral initial contact message using placeholders and no determination of motive or guilt.
4. Prepare the conversation opening, family questions, support options and an action table with owners and dates.
5. Document only agreed actions, preserve unresolved items and schedule policy-aligned follow-up.

Pass only the necessary previous **reviewed** output, not the entire conversation. Keep a local version label for each approved artifact. If an upstream fact changes, recheck every dependent artifact. Reset context between learner records.

## Fictional end-to-end example

Editorial worked illustration, not a recorded AI run or classroom test.

Inputs: Fictional routine interruption; role A spoke while role B was explaining; no safety concern supplied; motives unknown.

1. Record only the interruption; do not label intent.
2. Separate observation from any later reported account.
3. Draft: I would like to discuss the interruption and plan support for turn-taking.
4. Propose a listening question and a classroom rehearsal, not a punishment.
5. Keep agreements and follow-up blank until confirmed. Serious harm would stop this routine workflow.
