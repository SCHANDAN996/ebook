# Draft a measurable professional growth goal

## Use this when

You need a goal connected to learner evidence and realistic teacher actions.

## Teacher inputs

- `[FOCUS_AREA]`
- `[BASELINE_EVIDENCE]`
- `[DESIRED_OUTCOME]`
- `[TIMEFRAME]`

- `[EVIDENCE_SOURCES]`
- `[CONSTRAINTS]`

## Copy-paste prompt

```text
You are an experienced K-12 instructional planning assistant.

Teacher inputs:
- `[FOCUS_AREA]`
- `[BASELINE_EVIDENCE]`
- `[DESIRED_OUTCOME]`
- `[TIMEFRAME]`
- `[AVAILABLE_ACTIONS_OR_PD]`
- `[EVIDENCE_SOURCES]`
- `[CONSTRAINTS]`

Task:
Create a measurable growth plan without promising results outside the teacher's control.

Required output:
Return: goal statement; rationale; monthly actions; leading and outcome indicators; evidence collection; midpoint decision rules; support requested; reflection questions; privacy safeguards.

Rules:
- Use only the facts supplied. Mark missing essentials as [NEEDS TEACHER INPUT].
- Do not include identifiable student data or infer disability, motivation, family circumstances, or diagnosis.
- Keep the named grade, time, materials, objective, and policy constraints unchanged.
- Make student-facing language clear and age-appropriate.
- Check subject accuracy, feasibility, accessibility, and alignment before the final answer.
- End with a short TEACHER VERIFICATION checklist.
```

## Fictional test case

Increase quality of student mathematical explanations; baseline rubric average 1.8/4 across anonymous class work; one semester; weekly worked-example discussion.

Not included in this edition.

## Teacher verification checklist

- [ ] Every fact can be traced to the teacher inputs.
- [ ] Content, answers and examples are accurate.
- [ ] Timing, materials and difficulty are feasible.
- [ ] Accessibility supports preserve the learning goal.
- [ ] A teacher reviews the result before classroom or family use.
