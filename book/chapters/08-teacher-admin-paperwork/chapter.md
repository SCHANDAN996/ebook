# Chapter 8: Teacher Admin & Paperwork

Administrative writing consumes time because details, ownership and policy matter. These 25
prompts cover staff emails, agendas, minutes, professional goals, observation reflections,
field trips, clubs, duty plans, grants and proposals. Their purpose is to organize verified
information, not manufacture decisions or approvals.

## How to use this chapter

Paste rough notes only after removing confidential information. Mark confirmed decisions,
owners and deadlines; leave everything else visibly unresolved. When a document depends on
a local form or policy, provide that source or ask the model to insert `[CONFIRM]` instead
of guessing.

## Final review

Check names, dates, totals, links, responsibilities and approval routes. Confirm that meeting
minutes distinguish discussion from decision. For trips, grants and duty plans, use official
school and authority documents as the controlling source. Generated text may improve clarity,
but it cannot authorize spending, transport, supervision or legal compliance.
