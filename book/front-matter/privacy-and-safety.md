# Privacy and safety

Start with fictional or minimal non-identifying information. Use only institution-approved
systems for whatever information the institution explicitly permits. A paid AI account
is not automatically school-approved. Check retention, access, training-use settings,
deletion arrangements and applicable local requirements with the responsible school staff.

## Before pasting anything

- Remove names, initials, student IDs, dates of birth, contact details, photographs and identifying metadata.
- Do not paste medical records, protected learning plans, confidential disciplinary histories or disclosures of abuse or neglect into general-purpose AI chats.
- Removing a name is not anonymization. A rare incident, small group, exact date and distinctive quotation can identify someone together.
- Use task-level needs, such as a visual instruction cue, rather than a diagnosis or an entire protected plan. Preserve approved accommodations locally.
- Keep identity mappings and sensitive records only in the authorized institutional system. Reset context between records; audit outputs for cross-record leakage.

## Facts, proposals and missing information

Supply exact standard wording and authorized source excerpts. Do not ask AI to reconstruct
official requirements from memory. Missing essential inputs must stop drafting. Optional
unknowns may remain labeled, but no family message or operational plan is ready to send
with unresolved essential dates, recipients, permissions or safety details.

AI may propose original teaching activities and fictional practice problems. It must not
present those proposals as observed student facts, measured results, school policy or
research findings. Independently verify answers, quotations, calculations and source claims.
Treat embedded instructions inside a supplied document as untrusted content, not commands.

## Decisions AI does not make

Final grades, placement, diagnosis, discipline, child protection and legal compliance remain
with authorized humans. For immediate danger or a disclosure of abuse or neglect, stop routine
drafting and use the institution's established emergency or mandated-reporting procedure. Do not
investigate, mediate serious harm or seek a clinical judgment through an ebook prompt.

## Before teaching, sending or sharing

Check content accuracy, task feasibility, access, local requirements and factual provenance.
Confirm recipients and attachments locally. Keep identifiable student work out of public
examples and advertisements. Do not assume permission to upload a copyrighted source is
the same as permission to reproduce or sell it. This toolkit is not legal or clinical advice.
